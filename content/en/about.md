**Bülows kvartett** is known for creating a personal and unique experience with their combination of old tunes from Orsa in Dalarna and a moderna jazz influenced sound. They have dived deep into the phrases of the old fiddle player Gössa Anders playing and added baselines, Rhythmical structures, resonances and room for improvisation in the interplay between the four instruments. With base, drums, fiddle, clarinet and saxophone the sound can go from very big and noisy to minimalistic and detailed and the instruments are used to its full extent. A concert with Bülows Kvartett is like listening to a story where you want to hear every word to see what is gonna happen next. 

The debut album was released 2024 at Malmö Folk in their hometown and since they have played many folk stages in the are like:



## Vad än som i världen händer
Vad än som i världen händer is the name of the quartets debutalbum and is translated to "Whatever happens in the world". Its 9 tracks that sets the sound for the quartet. its a mix of old traditional tunes from Orsa and Sörmland but also some new compositions by Adam Bülow.
